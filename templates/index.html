About Us

A word that means a tale.

Vilas began with a simple idea — booking a room should feel as good as arriving at one. We curate a handful of stays in and around Kolhapur: a heritage kitchen where the tandoor is lit before sunrise, a palace wing with carved balconies, a hill retreat above Panhala's ramparts.

Every reservation here is transparent: you can hold a room without paying, see exactly how many rooms are reserved and booked across the property, and settle up with a simulated payment whenever you're ready. Cancel anytime — the room returns to the pool instantly.

And because a stay is half the story, you can order dinner from any hotel's kitchen — delivered to your door, or booked for the table with a date and time of your choosing.
